# Content & Social Media Guidelines (POSTS.md)

Guidelines and Tone of Voice for authoring articles, blog posts, and social media updates for Askold Astakhov.

---

## 1. Author Positioning & Core Philosophy

* **Who is writing:** Askold Astakhov - Frontend Tech Lead & Web Architect (React, Next.js, Web Architecture, AI & Agentic Workflows).
* **The Lens:** A hands-on technical leader who thinks in business outcomes. We do not just discuss syntax or libraries; we explain what business loses, risks, or gains through technical decisions.
* **Target Audience (ICP):**
  * **Digital Products:** CTOs, Heads of Engineering, Heads of Product, Technical Founders (struggling with tech debt, unpredicted delivery, contractor silos, scaling).
  * **Traditional Business:** COOs, CIOs, Digital Directors (struggling with legacy integration, multiple vendors, manual ops).
  * **Peer Community:** Senior engineers, tech leads, agency owners.

---

## 2. Universal "Anti-AI" Rules (Strict Compliance)

Any generated draft must pass these filters before publishing:

1. **NO Em-Dashes (`—`):** Never use em-dashes in drafts. Use standard hyphens `-`, colons, or clean sentence splits. Long em-dashes are an immediate AI giveaway.
2. **NO Symmetrical Antitheses ("Not A, but B"):** Avoid formulaic contrast patterns (*"It wasn't a code problem, it was a planning problem"*, *"Not because of X, but because of Y"*). Use natural, varied cadence.
3. **NO Preachy Moral Conclusions:** Never end a post with a neat, polished fable moral (*"Spend the budget where behavior changes"*, *"Nobody worked weekends"*). Leave conclusions practical or open for discussion.
4. **NO Sterile Story Arc:** Ban the cliché formula *"Client came wanting X -> Problem was actually Y -> We did Z -> Metric went up"*. Start directly with the paradox, the friction, or the operational reality.
5. **Include Raw Details & Reality:** Keep real constraints, trade-offs, organizational friction, and honest acknowledgments of limitations.

## 3. Platform Breakdown & Formats

### A. Blog on Site (`aastakhov.com`)
* **Role:** Single source of truth. Deep dives, case studies, and longreads.
* **Languages:** EN (primary) and RU (adapted).
* **Length:** 1,000 - 3,000 words.
* **Structure:** Real-world problem context -> technical breakdown (architecture diagrams, code patterns, benchmarks) -> business and operational impact.
* **SEO & Links:** Canonical source. Cross-posted to Medium/Dev.to with `canonical_url` pointing back to `aastakhov.com`.

---

### B. LinkedIn (EN)
* **Role:** Primary professional reputation and B2B client acquisition channel.
* **Language:** English only.
* **Cadence:** ~3 posts per week (optimal: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday).
* **Length:** 1,200 - 2,500 characters.
* **Format & Anatomy:**
  * **Hook (lines 1-3):** Sharp paradox, counterintuitive observation, or industry friction (visible before "...see more").
  * **Body:** Short paragraphs (1-3 sentences), plenty of whitespace. Focus on systems, cross-team handshakes, architecture, and team throughput.
  * **External Links:** Never put links in the post body (algorithms penalize reach). Always place links in the first comment.
  * **Outro:** End with an open-ended, authentic question addressed to peers (CTOs, Tech Leads, Engineering Managers) to spark comments.

---

### C. X.com / Twitter (EN)
* **Role:** High-density distribution and thought leadership for the global tech community.
* **Language:** English.
* **Format:**
  * **Standalone Takes (200 - 280 chars):** Concentrated punchlines, paradoxical truths, or sharp observations from engineering practice. Zero fluff.
  * **Threads (1/N):** First tweet acts as a high-friction hook. Followed by 3-5 concise breakdown tweets.
* **Hashtags & Links:** Do not use hashtags (considered noise/spam). Keep links off the primary tweet (drop in thread end or quote).

---

### D. Threads (RU - Community Experiment)
* **Role:** Informal community engagement, networking with Russian-speaking founders, leads, and developers.
* **Language:** Russian.
* **Format:**
  * **Short & Conversational:** 300 - 600 characters. Reads like a candid message in an engineering chat or over coffee.
  * **Content:** Real-life incidents, funny/painful process absurdities, relatable team moments, and provocative questions.
  * **Interaction-Driven:** Must end with a low-friction question that takes 5 seconds to answer from a smartphone.
  * **Topics/Tags:** Use native tags like `#it`, `#разработка`, `#управление` or publish tagless relying on semantic search. Never use broad English tags like `Business` or `Technology`.
